The public homepage and auth copy follow `docs/specs/website-family-copy.md` and `docs/specs/website-family-layout.md`. Product purpose comes from `docs/product-vision.md`; current behavior is checked against the handlers and focused tests below. The copy makes no measured outcome, automatic-execution, real-time synchronization, pricing, approval or security guarantee.
